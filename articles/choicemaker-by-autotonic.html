Life is about making choices. ChoiceMaker helps you train the next one.

多功能隨機決策與訓練助手

ChoiceMaker by AutoTonic turns your iPhone into a hands-free random choice trainer, virtual dice, timer, coach, and scorekeeper. Create your own choice sets, let the app call the next task, move through timed practice rounds, and track how many reps you scored. Use it for sports drills, coaching sessions, classroom prompts, creative warmups, language practice, music exercises, professional training, hobbies, games, routines, or any practice where surprise makes you sharper. What ChoiceMaker can do: Build custom sets of choices for your own tasks, drills, prompts, or actions. Run a single random choice or combine multiple sets into sequence-style results. Adjust the probability of each choice so important items appear more often. Use pre-delay and timer settings to prepare, react, repeat, and keep moving. Control sessions with voice commands when your hands are busy. Let iOS speak the result, or record your own audio cue for each choice. Enable scoring to mark successful reps and review how your session went. Use challenge timing for focused performance rounds. Save complete templates and presets so your favorite setups are ready again. Start quickly from Siri Shortcuts and App Intents. A few examples: Boxing: combine direction, technique, and reaction callouts. Tennis: randomize target zones, footwork, and shot types. Fitness: call the next station, movement, or intensity. Music: practice scales, rhythms, chords, or improvisation constraints. Teaching: pick questions, roles, groups, or topics. Work: rehearse sales objections, presentation prompts, or response drills. ChoiceMaker is intentionally flexible. It does not decide what matters for you. It gives you a reliable way to create structured randomness, repeat it, score it, and improve with it. Create a set. Say "Next". Do the task. Score the rep. Repeat.
